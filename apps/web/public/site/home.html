How it works

Four weeks from signed contract to live calls.

Our team sits with yours to turn a working sales process into a working voice agent. No stock scripts; nothing goes live until it passes a pre-launch check.

01
Week 1 · Discover

Sit in on your sales floor

Shadow a day of calls, read the CRM, meet the humans the agent will stand in for.

  • Record the current playbook
  • Agree on goals, hours and limits
  • Pick a pilot audience
02
Week 2 · Design

Author the agent

A versioned conversation workflow — qualification questions, objection handling, handoff rules.

  • Voice, language and tone
  • Actions the agent may take alone
  • When to pass to a human
03
Week 3 · Connect

Wire up the systems

Upload the lead list, pick the caller numbers, map lead statuses to campaigns — tested on real contacts before anything goes out.

  • Excel import or POST /leads
  • Status filters drive the campaign
  • Pre-launch compliance check
04
Week 4 · Launch

Dial, watch, tune

Start small, grow the volume. Every call is recorded, transcribed, and reviewable the next morning.

  • Emergency stop at your fingertips
  • Daily call reviews with your SDRs
  • Weekly agent revisions

Lead sources & integrations

Bring your leads in the way that fits — then let status drive the dial.

Upload a spreadsheet or call our API. However the lead arrives, every number is checked against DNC and DND before Nivomi rings it.

  • Excel & CSV uploadColumn mapping, duplicate review, chunked import. Up to tens of thousands of rows per file.
  • Direct REST APIPOST /leads with Idempotency-Key for your website form or back-office tool.
  • Status-driven campaignsSave a campaign once; every lead that enters NEW, FOLLOW_UP or CALLBACK_REQUESTED is dialled automatically.
  • Signed webhooksReceive events from Nivomi for your back-office automation.
