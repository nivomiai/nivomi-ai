## Claims

1. **The 2025 amendment.** On 12 Feb 2025, TRAI notified the TCCCPR (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2025 (1 of 2025), which amend the principal TCCCPR 2018 (6 of 2018).
   - Commencement: 30 days after Gazette publication, except reg 8, reg 17, sub-clauses (a) and (b) of reg 20, and sub-clause (b) of reg 21. Those start 60 days after publication (cl. 1(3)).
   - Source: S1 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
   - Two-source: single-source for the text. Search snippets from Mondaq/JSA/Securiti cite the same date but were not read.

2. **"Promotional Voice Call" is redefined (reg 2(av)).** It means "any voice Commercial Communication containing promotional material or advertisement of a product or service".
   - "if promotional content is mixed with any type of commercial Voice Call, such voice call shall be treated as a Promotional Voice Call."
   - Such calls reach only subscribers who have not blocked the category in the Preference Register, or who have consented in the Consent Register.
   - With "explicit digital consent", the call is delivered "irrespective of their preferences registered".
   - Source: S1 cl. 2(e) · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class definition
   - Two-source: single-source

3. **Inference, not verbatim: AI sales and lead-qualification calls are Promotional Voice Calls.** They advertise a product or service, and any promotional pitch mixed into a service-type call turns the whole call promotional.
   - Source: derived from S1 cl. 2(e)–(g) · confidence medium · class definition
   - Two-source: single-source (interpretation)

4. **"Service Voice Call" is redefined (reg 2(bh)).** It covers two cases:
   - (i) calls to the sender's own Customer about a product or service they use (warranty, recall, delivery, etc.). These are "not promotional in nature and do not require Explicit Consent".
   - (ii) calls to a Recipient "to facilitate or complete a commercial transaction involving the ongoing purchase" after obtaining Explicit Consent. For case (ii), "such Explicit Consent shall be for seven days or as directed by the Authority".
   - Source: S1 cl. 2(f) · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class definition
   - Two-source: single-source

5. **"Transactional Voice Call" is narrowed (reg 2(bt)).** It is a call to the sender's Customer "in response to Customer initiated transaction within thirty minutes of the transaction" (OTPs, transaction alerts, refunds, etc.). Clause (bu) was deleted.
   - Source: S1 cl. 2(g)–(h) · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class definition
   - Two-source: single-source

6. **Inferred consent is time-limited.** It "shall not extend beyond duration / discharge of the contract between the Sender and the Recipient". Per the memorandum, this replaces an earlier 12-month notion.
   - Source: S1 cl. 2(c) and EM para 14 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
   - Two-source: single-source

7. **UCC definition (reg 2(bw)).**
   - Any commercial communication "neither as per the consent nor the registered preferences of the Recipient" is Unsolicited Commercial Communication (UCC). Transactional, service and government calls are excluded.
   - "any commercial communication made by a Sender which is not registered with any Access Provider ... shall be treated as" UCC.
   - Deceptive calls are also treated as UCC.
   - Source: S1 cl. 2(i) · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
   - Two-source: single-source

8. **Reg 3 as substituted: 10-digit numbers are effectively barred for commercial calls.**
   - Access Providers must ensure commercial communication uses "only ... registered headers or the number resources allotted to the Senders from special series assigned for the purpose of commercial communication".
   - Unregistered Senders "shall [not] make any commercial communication". Their telecom resources may be suspended or disconnected.
   - Source: S1 cl. 3 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
   - Two-source: single-source for the text. The MEF/Securiti search snippets say the same ("restrict the use of regular 10-digit numbers") but were not read.

9. **Reg 4 as substituted: auto-dialer and robocall notice.** "Every Sender shall notify the Originating Access Provider, in advance, about the use of Auto Dialer or Robo-Calls as well as the intended objective of such calls in writing."
   - Per the memorandum, promotional auto-dialer or robo calls are permitted "through 140-series numbers only", and service or transactional ones through 1600 or another allotted series.
   - Per the memorandum, promotional robo-calls should use the existing DCA platforms for consent.
   - TRAI decided that no separate robocall regulation is needed (EM paras 18, 50, 52).
   - Source: S1 cl. 4 and EM · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
   - Two-source: single-source (the Securiti snippet agrees but was not read)

10. **Number series: 140 is for promotional calls, 160/1600 for service and transactional.**
    - TRAI states that "promotional call should be permitted through 140-series numbers only" and that "DoT has recently allotted 1600 series numbers for making transactional and service calls" (S1 EM para 50).
    - PR 58/2024 says "160 series has been allocated exclusively for Service and Transactional Calls" and that SIP/PRI users "should be migrated to the designated 140 series for making promotional calls" (S2).
    - Sources: S1, S2 · TRAI · 2025-02 / 2024-08-27 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-publisher (two TRAI documents). No DoT source was read.

11. **Promotional calls from the 1600/service series: complaint-triggered sanctions on the Sender (reg 25, as substituted by amending reg 8; numbering inferred).**
    - Trigger: complaints from at least 5 unique recipients in the last 10 days. The Originating Access Provider (OAP) checks within 2 business hours, then suspends the resources and investigates within 5 business days.
    - First violation: "outgoing services of all the telecom resources of the Sender including PRI/SIP trunks etc shall be barred by all the Access Providers for a period of fifteen days".
    - Second violation: all resources disconnected across all providers for one year, the Sender is blacklisted with no new resources, and all devices used are blocked for one year. One number may be retained.
    - Representation to the OAP is decided within 7 business days, with appeal to TRAI (reg 29).
    - Source: S1 cl. 8 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high (text); medium (clause numbering) · class enforcement
    - Two-source: single-source

12. **Complaints about unregistered telemarketers (UTMs) use the same 5-complainants-in-10-days threshold.**
    - The OAP checks call detail records (CDRs) within 1 business day, checks for similar complaints within 2 business hours, then suspends all of the Sender's telecom resources and investigates.
    - Complaints must be lodged within 7 days of receiving the communication.
    - Customers need not have registered a preference to complain against a UTM.
    - Source: S1 cl. 6–8 and EM para 20 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class enforcement
    - Two-source: single-source

13. **Restoring resources costs the Sender money.** When TRAI orders restoration on appeal, the Sender pays "a restoration charge of five thousand rupees per resource", capped at ₹5 lakh.
    - "in the case of PRI or SIP trunks, each DID number shall be treated as a separate telecom resource" (reg 29).
    - Source: S1 cl. 13 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class enforcement
    - Two-source: single-source

14. **Sender and telemarketer registration and annual obligations.**
    - Registration requires "(a) physical verification of the entity; (b) biometric authentication of the authorized person ...; (c) linking of the entity with a unique mobile number" (Sched-I item 1(4)).
    - Senders and registered telemarketers (RTMs) must self-certify annually their registration details and "all of their registered headers, content templates and Consent Templates". Failure means "automatic suspension" (reg 22(1)(c)).
    - Source: S1 cl. 5, 17 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-source

15. **Traceability and chain limits (reg 22(1)(e)).** There may be no more than two telemarketers in a chain: "one Telemarketer with Aggregator Function and one Telemarketer with Delivery Function".
    - The Sender's agreement must make it "the sole responsibility of the Sender" to use only its registered headers or its allotted special-series numbers, "and no promotional content shall be mixed in it" (reg 22(1)(i)(i)(A)).
    - PR 58/2024 also pushes "declaration of entire chain of telemarketers engaged by them for PE-TM chain binding" (principal entity to telemarketer).
    - Sources: S1 cl. 5; S2 · TRAI · 2025-02 / 2024-08 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-publisher

16. **Consent mechanics (Sched-I item 4(2)(g)–(k), inserted by reg 17).**
    - "the Sender shall be allowed to re-acquire consent of such customer only after ninety (90) days from the date of revoking consent or opting-out".
    - Consent-seeking messages must come from "short code 127xxx, or any other code as prescribed by the Authority".
    - Customers can register unwillingness to receive any consent-seeking message via SMS, IVR or online.
    - The "scope and the name of the Principal Entity/brand" must be stated in the consent-seeking message.
    - The consent confirmation must include revocation information.
    - Source: S1 cl. 17 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-source

17. **Consents lapse when a number is surrendered.** "whenever a telecom resource is surrendered or closed, all the Consents registered against that telecom resource shall be revoked on the DL- Consent", the DLT consent register (Sched-II item 8, reg 18(c)). Preferences reset to default in the same case.
    - Source: S1 cl. 18(b)–(c) · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-source

18. **Digital Consent Acquisition (DCA) is live and covers voice.** TRAI calls DCA "of great value to the entities, not only for messaging services, but also for voice calls. It permits the delivery of Messages and calls to the recipients despite their DND preference. The technical infrastructure for DCA is now in place" (S2).
    - The memorandum describes DCA as enabling acquisition, and customer revocation, of consent to receive commercial communication (S1 EM).
    - Sources: S2, S1 · TRAI · 2024-08-27 / 2025-02 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-publisher

19. **Preference categories (Sched-II item 1(1) as substituted).** Customers can choose:
    - FULLY BLOCK (*1909*0#): blocks all communication except transactional and service with inferred consent, and government.
    - BLOCK PROMO (*1909*50#).
    - Eight content categories (*1909*1#–8#): 1 Banking/Insurance/Financial, 2 Real Estate, 3 Education, 4 Health, 5 Consumer goods and automobiles, 6 Communication/Broadcasting/Entertainment/IT, 7 Tourism and Leisure, 8 Food and Beverages.
    - FULLY BLOCK also blocks "service types ... requiring Explicit Consent".
    - Source: S1 cl. 18(a) · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-source

20. **Carrier-side spam detection signals (Sched-IV item 1(1)(g), UCC_Detect).** Senders above a call-volume threshold are screened for:
    - high daily B-number diversity (many distinct called numbers);
    - average call duration below a prescribed value;
    - incoming-to-outgoing call ratio below a prescribed value;
    - devices hosting 4 or more MSISDNs in a month.
    - Providers must also detect "robotic calls, auto dialer calls or pre-recorded announcements, SIM Farm/SIM box", use AI/ML, and deploy honeypots (1 per 500 complaints, minimum 10 per licensed service area) that record voice calls.
    - Relevance: high-volume AI dialing from ordinary numbers is exactly what these signals target.
    - Source: S1 cl. 20 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class gatekeeper-policy
    - Two-source: single-source

21. **Reg 34A: call-management apps may not block the designated series.** "No Call Management Application ... shall tag, block, filter, or restrict incoming calls ... originating from the designated number series assigned for commercial communications".
    - The memorandum's rationale: blanket 140-series blocking "drives legitimate telemarketers to use unauthorized 10-digit numbers".
    - Source: S1 cl. 16 and EM para 45 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class gatekeeper-policy
    - Two-source: single-source

22. **TRAI Direction of 13 Aug 2024 on UTM voice calls.** It ordered that "All promotional voice calls from unregistered senders or UTMs using telecom resources such as ... SIP, PRI and other telecom resources, must cease immediately".
    - Violators face disconnection of all telecom resources for up to two years and blacklisting by the OAP for the same period.
    - Access Providers must share blacklisting on DLT within 24 hours, disconnect within 24 hours, and report on the 1st and 16th of each month.
    - Source: S3 · Khaitan & Co (Mondaq) · 2024-08-30 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence medium-high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-source (read). The Communications Today, ETV Bharat and Tribune snippets agree but were not read, and the TRAI Direction itself was not read.

23. **Reported enforcement after the Aug 2024 direction.** Access Providers "blacklisted over 50 entities and disconnected more than 2.75 lakh SIP DID/Mobile Numbers/Telecom resources".
    - Source: search snippet attributed to TRAI/Tribune coverage (not read) · 2024-09 · confidence low · class enforcement / quantitative
    - Two-source: single-source, unread

24. **TRAI regards SIP/PRI commercial calling as a violation.** "Many business entities make commercial voice calls using SIP/ PRI lines ... in violation of TRAI's regulations. These entities should be migrated to the designated 140 series".
    - TRAI also called for "firm action ... on spammers who are using PRI/ SIP/ bulk connections for making promotional voice calls/ Robo calls/ Pre-recorded calls".
    - Source: S2 · TRAI · 2024-08-27 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class enforcement
    - Two-source: confirmed in substance by #22 (S3, different publisher)

25. **1600-series Direction for BFSI (banking, financial services and insurance).** On 19 Nov 2025 (PR No. 135/2025), TRAI issued a Direction mandating phase-wise adoption of the 1600 series by BFSI entities regulated by RBI, SEBI and PFRDA.
    - Deadlines from search snippets, not verified in the Direction text: scheduled commercial banks by 1 Jan 2026; large NBFCs, payments banks and small finance banks by 1 Feb 2026; MFs/AMCs by 15 Feb 2026; remaining NBFCs, co-operative banks and RRBs by 1 Mar 2026.
    - A separate Direction covers IRDAI-regulated entities (PIB PRID 2205350, fetch blocked with HTTP 403).
    - Sources: S4 (existence and date) plus search snippets (DNA India, Mondaq; deadlines) · TRAI · 2025-11-19 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high (existence), low (deadlines) · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-source for the deadlines

26. **What the regulator fines, and the cap.** TRAI's financial disincentives under regs 27–28 fall on Access Providers, capped at ₹50 lakh per month per licensed service area (reg 28A). Examples: ₹1,000 per complaint wrongly declared invalid, ₹5,000 per bad header or template registration, ₹2–10 lakh for misreporting.
    - Senders and telemarketers face resource suspension, disconnection, blacklisting, forfeiture of security deposits, and financial disincentives that Access Providers impose "at its discretion" (reg 22(1)(g)).
    - Source: S1 cl. 5, 10–12 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class enforcement
    - Two-source: single-source

27. **Header suffixes apply to SMS headers, not voice numbers.** Headers must be identifiable by "-P", "-S", "-T" and "-G" suffixes for Promotional, Service, Transactional and Government (Sched-I item 2).
    - Source: S1 cl. 17 · TRAI · 2025-02-12 · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence high · class regulatory
    - Two-source: single-source

## Glossary

- **TCCCPR 2018**: Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018 (6 of 2018). TRAI's UCC framework, amended by 1 of 2025. (S1)
- **Sender / Principal Entity (PE)**: the business on whose behalf commercial communication is sent. It registers headers, content templates and consent templates, and is solely responsible for using only its registered headers or allotted series numbers. (S1 reg 22(1)(i), Sched-I 4(2)(j))
- **RTM / UTM**: Registered Telemarketer, or Unregistered Telemarketer or unregistered sender. Commercial communication from an unregistered Sender is UCC per se. (S1 reg 2(bw), reg 3)
- **Telemarketer with Aggregator Function / Delivery Function**: the at most two intermediaries allowed between the Sender and the Access Provider. (S1 reg 22(1)(e))
- **OAP / TAP**: Originating Access Provider (the Sender's carrier, which acts on complaints) and Terminating Access Provider (the complainant's carrier). (S1)
- **DLT**: distributed-ledger platforms run by Access Providers for registering entities, headers, templates, consent (DL-Consent) and preferences (DL-Preference). (S1)
- **DCA**: Digital Consent Acquisition, the Access-Provider platform for acquiring and revoking consent. Consent-seeking messages come from short code 127xxx. (S1, S2)
- **Explicit consent**: consent recorded via the prescribed digital mechanism. It lets promotional calls through despite DND preferences; for service case (ii) it is valid for 7 days. (S1)
- **Inferred consent**: consent reasonably inferred from the customer relationship, valid only for the duration of the contract. (S1)
- **Promotional / Service / Transactional / Government Voice Call**: see claims 2, 4 and 5; reg 2(za). (S1)
- **UCC**: Unsolicited Commercial Communication. (S1 reg 2(bw))
- **140-series**: numbers designated for promotional voice calls. (S1 EM, S2)
- **160 / 1600-series**: numbers allotted by DoT for service and transactional voice calls. (S1 EM, S2, S4)
- **UCC_Detect**: carrier systems that flag suspected UTMs from traffic signals. (S1 Sched-IV)
- **Preference Register / NCPR (DND)**: customer block preferences such as FULLY BLOCK, BLOCK PROMO and the 8 categories, set via 1909. (S1 Sched-II)

## Leads

**Contradictions and tensions (first):**

- **L1: 160 vs 1600 naming.** S2 (Aug 2024) says "160 series"; S1 and S4 (2025) say "1600". Probably 1600xx is a sub-series of 160 (the S1 memorandum also mentions "160xx"/"160xxx"). A DoT allotment order is needed to settle it.
- **L2: 7-day complaint window vs threshold-based action.** Complaints must be lodged within 7 days, while sanctions trigger at 5 unique complainants in 10 days. Check the Codes of Practice for how these interact.
- **L3: Where do AI lead-qualification calls sit?** Service case (ii) (explicit consent, 7-day validity, "ongoing purchase") versus Promotional. The inference is promotional, but an inbound-lead callback could be argued as service (ii). TRAI FAQs or clarifications are needed.

**Open threads:**

- **L4:** Read the principal TCCCPR 2018 text for voice calling-hour restrictions (the unverified belief is 09:00–21:00 for promotional calls) and the "Consent Template" definition. The 2025 amendment text did not restate calling hours.
- **L5:** Is there a TCCCPR "First Amendment" (the 2025 instrument is "Second")? Also find the date of Gazette publication, which the effective dates run from.
- **L6:** Find the TRAI 13 Aug 2024 Direction PDF itself (not read) and the TRAI Direction or deadline for migrating promotional-call telemarketers to 140-series on DLT (PR 58/2024 refers to "timelines fixed by TRAI's latest Directions").
- **L7:** Read the 1600-series Directions (BFSI 19 Nov 2025; IRDAI via PIB PRID 2205350) and any extension to other sectors or to all service/transactional callers in 2026. Also look for DoT orders allotting 1600 and 140 series.
- **L8:** Operator DLT portal documentation (Jio/Airtel/Vi/BSNL) on how voice is registered (calling-number or 140 DID registration against the principal entity, voice content templates, PE-TM chain binding for voice) and on how a CPaaS/SaaS acting as a telemarketer registers.
- **L9:** 2025–2026 TRAI consultations after this amendment, e.g. on CNAP, AI voice calls, or a TCCCPR "Third Amendment". None were found this round.
- **L10:** TRAI's "prescribed thresholds" for UCC_Detect signals (call volume, B-number diversity, minimum average duration) are set by direction. Find them, because they determine whether an AI dialer gets flagged.
- **L11:** Secondary analyses (JSA, Securiti, Sigma Chambers, indialaw.in) were seen in search but not read. They are useful to corroborate claims 1–9.
