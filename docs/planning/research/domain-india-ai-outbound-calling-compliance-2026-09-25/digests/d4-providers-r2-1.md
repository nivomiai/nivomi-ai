# Digest d4-providers-r2-1: Exotel and Sarvam provider responsibilities and data terms (round 2)

Run: domain research, India AI outbound calling compliance. Accessed: 2026-09-25 for all sources.
Tool budget used: 18 calls (1 tool load, 15 search/fetch, 1 write, 1 hand-off). 12 distinct sources consulted (8 retrieved directly, the rest seen only as search snippets).
Caveat: the Exotel developer-docs pages (developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/*) show **no publication or last-updated date**, so their freshness cannot be checked. All Exotel and Sarvam claims come from the providers themselves: two pages from the same publisher do **not** count as independent sources. Page contents were read through a summarising fetch tool, so the "quotes" are near-verbatim, not exact.

## Claims

### Q1: Exotel voice compliance

1. Exotel's developer docs say "DLT registration is only required for SMS. Voice calls do not require DLT templates or Entity IDs." They add that voice campaigns must still follow other TRAI rules (calling hours, DND filtering). Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/dlt-compliance · Exotel · undated · accessed 2026-09-25 · confidence LOW-MEDIUM (primary but undated, and it conflicts with claim 3 and with TRAI's 140-series DLT regime; see Leads) · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
   - Round-1 hypothesis "customers register themselves as Principal Entity on DLT (pages covered only SMS)": **STILL SINGLE-SOURCE / SMS-ONLY**. No Exotel page found says what a customer registers on DLT for voice. No Exotel page says whether Exotel registers as the telemarketer on the customer's behalf.
2. Exotel's TCCCPR page lists these as customer duties: register on DLT portals, configure account settings, keep consent records with timestamps, honour opt-outs, and "enable DND filtering for promotional campaigns". It describes Exotel's own role as supplying the platform and doing "real-time content matching" (SMS-oriented). Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/tcccpr-2018 · Exotel · undated (titled "TCCCPR 2018") · confidence MEDIUM for how Exotel divides responsibility, LOW for whether it is current · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
3. An Exotel blog (19 Nov 2024, Vivek Jathanna) says Exotel recommends 140-series calling for promotion-heavy use cases that draw DND complaints. It also says that without DND complaints, organisations "may continue to use SDCA (City / Normal number) for outbound calling", and that TRAI's final 160-series directions "are yet to be released". Search snippets of Exotel pages also say "Exotel does not allow cold calling", that promotional calls use a 140-series number, and that "DLT registration number is mandatory for 140 series numbers". The snippets could not be pinned to a single Exotel URL, and one of them conflicts with claim 1. Source: https://exotel.com/blog/decoding-new-calling-regulations-of-140-160-calling/ · Exotel · 2024-11-19 · confidence LOW (older than the 18-month freshness limit; written before TRAI's 2025 amendment) · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
   - Round-1 hypothesis "2024 blog said city numbers remained usable (likely stale)": **STILL SINGLE-SOURCE, NOT OVERTURNED BY AN EXOTEL SOURCE**. The statement is confirmed as written, but no newer Exotel page was found that either keeps or withdraws it. Treat it as stale.
4. Exotel's own docs do not describe provisioning 1600/160-series numbers to customers. The number-types FAQ covers only landline DIDs, virtual mobile numbers, toll-free and vanity numbers, and has no compliance content. Search snippets say toll-free (1800) numbers cannot place outbound calls. Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/number-types · Exotel · undated · confidence MEDIUM (for what the page does not contain) · definition · SINGLE-SOURCE.
5. Exotel says it "integrates with the NDNC database and provides DND filtering at multiple stages", including "at campaign execution: numbers are filtered in real time before calls are placed". It says promotional voice calls to DND numbers are blocked, transactional voice calls are "generally allowed", and service calls to existing customers are allowed "with implicit consent". The page does not say whether filtering is on by default or needs a flag. Claim 2's "enable DND filtering" wording suggests the customer must turn it on. Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/ndnc-dnd · Exotel · undated · confidence MEDIUM-LOW (the "implicit consent" language may predate the 2025 amendment) · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
6. Exotel's TCCCPR page gives "9 AM – 9 PM IST" as the calling window for commercial promotional calls. It lists penalties of "up to INR 1,000 per message/call per day" for unsolicited commercial communication (UCC) sent to DND numbers, and "disconnection of telecom resources for up to 2 years" after 7 or more complaints in a month. Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/tcccpr-2018 · Exotel · undated · confidence LOW (the page is framed around the 2018 regulation; these figures must be checked against the TRAI primary text and the 2025 amendment before any reliance) · regulatory (as reported by the provider) · SINGLE-SOURCE.
7. No Exotel page found mentions auto-dialer, robocall, pre-recorded or AI-call declarations, or any compliance announcement from after 2025. Nothing retrieved.

### Q2: Exotel data

8. Exotel says data is "stored exclusively in the region selected during account creation" with "no automatic cross-region replication". The regions are Mumbai (AWS ap-south-1) and Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1). Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/data-privacy · Exotel · undated · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE. A search snippet from the Exotel call-recording docs agrees: recordings are kept in Exotel cloud storage (AWS S3) in the account's region, and enterprise accounts can use their own cloud storage. Same publisher, so not independent.
9. Exotel's default retention periods: call recordings 90 days (configurable by plan), CDRs 1 year (not configurable), SMS delivery logs 1 year, API access logs 90 days, webhook logs 30 days. Longer retention is arranged through the account manager. Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/data-privacy · Exotel · undated · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
10. Exotel describes no self-service deletion API. Individual recordings are deleted by contacting support, and full account deletion is requested by email (hello@exotel.com). Data marked for deletion is "typically purged within 30 days". Source: same page · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
11. A Data Processing Agreement is available **on request for enterprise customers**. It covers processing scope, notice of subprocessor changes, and breach procedures, with breach notification "within 72 hours". No public subprocessor list and no DPDP Act statement were found. Encryption is TLS 1.2+ in transit and AES-256 at rest. Source: same page · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.

### Q3: Exotel voicebot / streaming (AgentStream)

12. The AgentStream WebSocket audio format is raw PCM linear16: 16-bit signed little-endian, mono, base64-encoded. The default sample rate is 8,000 Hz; 16,000 Hz and 24,000 Hz are also supported. Chunks are 3,200–100,000 bytes, in multiples of 320. Source: https://developer.exotel.com/docs/agentstream/websocket-protocol · Exotel · undated · confidence MEDIUM-HIGH (primary technical doc) · definition · SINGLE-SOURCE.
    - Round-1 hypothesis "8 kHz 16-bit PCM": **CONFIRMED AND REFINED**. 8 kHz is the default, not the only rate.
13. The "Stream" applet is one-way (Exotel sends audio to the server). The "Voicebot" applet is two-way on the same WebSocket. Exotel sends connected, start, media, dtmf (Voicebot only), mark (Voicebot only) and stop events. The server can send media, mark and clear; clear flushes buffered, not-yet-played audio, which is how barge-in works. Source: same page · confidence MEDIUM-HIGH · definition · SINGLE-SOURCE.
14. The maximum stream session length is 60 minutes. The protocol page does not state timeouts or concurrency limits. Source: same page · confidence MEDIUM-HIGH · definition · SINGLE-SOURCE.
    - Round-1 hypothesis "60-minute session cap": **CONFIRMED** (same publisher as round 1). "10-second bot timeout": **STILL SINGLE-SOURCE** (not on the protocol page).
15. The protocol page does not describe a way to transfer a streaming call to a human number. It points only to "Connect with Flow" and "Programmable Voice (ExoML)". The stream/voicebot applet guides (support.exotel.com article 3000108630, and the updated extension guide 3000132302, marked Beta) were found but not fetched. Confidence LOW · definition · NOT FOUND in the retrieved text.

### Q4: Sarvam AI

16. Sarvam's Terms of Service (last updated 2026-07-29) say that where Sarvam uses Inputs, Outputs or usage data to train its models, it does so under the Privacy Policy and applicable law, "(where required) subject to your consent, which you may decline or withdraw". Declining may restrict access to "certain Offerings". Anonymised, aggregated data becomes Sarvam IP and may be used in marketing. Source: https://www.sarvam.ai/terms-of-service · Sarvam AI · 2026-07-29 · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE (Sarvam only).
17. Sarvam's Privacy Policy says it uses content (inputs, uploads, prompts, outputs) to train or fine-tune models **unless the user opts out**, through Account Settings or by emailing Sarvam. This comes from a search snippet of https://www.sarvam.ai/privacy-policy and matches round 1. Sarvam AI · 2026-07-29 (per round 1) · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
    - Round-1 hypothesis "trains on customer content unless opt-out": **CONFIRMED for the Privacy Policy**, but the Terms frame training as consent-based (claim 16). See Leads.
18. The Terms say the DPA applies when Customers use the Offerings to process personal data on their behalf: "the Customer acts as controller and Sarvam as processor". The DPA is incorporated by reference, but its text was not found publicly. Snippets say processing follows the Privacy Policy and applicable law, including the DPDP Act, 2023. Source: https://www.sarvam.ai/terms-of-service · 2026-07-29 · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
19. The Terms say: "We process and store information in India and may transfer it to other countries where we and our service providers operate." Source: https://www.sarvam.ai/terms-of-service · 2026-07-29 · confidence MEDIUM · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
    - Round-1 hypothesis "possible transfer to US/EU": **CONFIRMED IN PRINCIPLE** (cross-border transfer is allowed). The Terms name no countries; round 1's US/EU detail rests on the Privacy Policy alone.
20. The Terms specify no retention period and offer no zero-data-retention option; retention is deferred to the Privacy Policy. Source: same · confidence MEDIUM (for what the page does not contain).
    - Round-1 hypothesis "default retention 30 days": **STILL SINGLE-SOURCE** (Privacy Policy only).
21. The Terms prohibit "unsolicited commercial communications, promotional or transactional calls or messages in violation of applicable law, including the Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations". They also prohibit recording, monitoring or transcribing conversations without the notice or consent the law requires, and cloning any person's voice without explicit, informed and, where required, written consent. Source: same · confidence HIGH (explicit clause) · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
22. Sarvam's aggregate liability is capped at the greater of the fees paid for the product in the prior 12 months or INR 1,000. The Terms are governed by Indian law, with a 60-day informal resolution period, then arbitration in Bengaluru, and no class actions. Source: same · confidence HIGH · gatekeeper-policy · SINGLE-SOURCE.
23. The Terms say some plans or features are limited to Customers and point to "Product-Specific Terms", but give no data-handling differences between free, API, paid or enterprise tiers. Source: same · confidence MEDIUM.
    - Round-1 contradiction "no training only for paid/custom engagements": **UNRESOLVED**. The Terms neither confirm nor rule out tier-based training exemptions.

## Leads

1. **CONTRADICTION (Exotel, voice DLT):** Exotel's dev docs say voice needs no DLT (claim 1). Exotel-attributed snippets say a DLT registration number is mandatory for 140-series numbers (claim 3), and the TCCCPR page tells customers to register on DLT portals (claim 2). Exotel's docs are internally inconsistent and undated. TRAI or telco primary sources on voice DLT and 140-series header registration are needed to settle it.
2. **CONTRADICTION (Sarvam, training basis):** the Terms (2026-07-29) frame training on Inputs and Outputs as "(where required) subject to your consent". The Privacy Policy (same date) frames it as default-on unless the user opts out. For a B2B controller–processor setup, a processor training on controller data by default is a material issue. Needed: the DPA text itself, and written confirmation from Sarvam of whether API/Customer accounts are excluded from training by default.
3. **CONTRADICTION (Exotel calling hours and penalties vs the regulator):** Exotel's "9 AM – 9 PM" window and its "7+ complaints → up to 2 years disconnection" figure come from a page framed on TCCCPR 2018. They need checking against TRAI's primary text and the 2025 amendment.
4. Exotel says DND filtering happens "in real time before calls are placed" (claim 5), but also lists "enable DND filtering" as a customer duty (claim 2). It is unclear whether filtering is on by default or opt-in. The platform should not assume it is on by default.
5. The Exotel stream/voicebot applet guides (support.exotel.com/.../3000108630 and 3000132302, marked "Beta") likely cover transfer to the next applet or a human, the 10-second timeout, and custom parameters. Fetch them next round.
6. The Exotel DPA is "on request for enterprise customers". It is unknown whether a non-enterprise account can get one; ask Exotel sales. The subprocessor list is not public.
7. The Sarvam DPA and "Product-Specific Terms" are referenced but were not located. Try sarvam.ai/dpa, sarvam.ai/eula, or a trust/security page.
