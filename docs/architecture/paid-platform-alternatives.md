# Paid platforms and their alternatives

For each place where Nivomi could pay a vendor, this page records what is built today, the paid
options, and what we would self-host instead. It helps the owner decide **when** a paid service
earns its cost.

**No prices are listed on purpose.** Vendor pricing changes often and must be read from each
vendor's official pricing page, in INR where offered, on the day of the decision. Record the
figures and the date in the ADR that makes the choice.

## How to use this page

1. When a trigger fires, open an ADR (or update the one listed).
2. Read current pricing and limits from the vendor's official pages; note the date and region.
3. Estimate cost per call and per lead at pilot volume and at 100× (PRD §13).
4. Record data-residency and DPDP implications (processor location, sub-processors).
5. Keep the provider behind its interface (`EmailProvider`, `VoiceProvider`, …) so switching stays a
   configuration and adapter change.
